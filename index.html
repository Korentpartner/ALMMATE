Talk to a real student, not a counsellor

You should never have to just take our word for it. Check every mentor yourself in minutes.

CHECK 1

LinkedIn profile

  • Ask the mentor for their LinkedIn profile URL
  • Check that the university appears under Education
  • Confirm the course years match their claims
  • Look for agency/broker jobs in their experience
  • Check their connections and profile age
CHECK 2

University ID card

  • Ask a current student to show their university ID
  • Match the name & college on the card with LinkedIn
  • Alumni can show a degree or mark sheet
  • Mentors may blur private enrollment roll numbers
CHECK 3

Quick video call

  • Meet on a short 5-minute video call
  • Ask them to show their student ERP dashboard live
  • Ask specific questions only real students know
  • Discuss LMS, exam proctoring, and professors
CHECK 4

Verified badge

  • Our team audits the same proofs before badge issue
  • Verified Student or Verified Alumni tags
  • Unverified profiles are clearly flagged
  • Community report button available on all profiles

🚩 Warning signs of a fake counsellor

  • Asks you for money, a registration token, or a private Google Pay link. ALMMATE mentors never charge.
  • Refuses to share LinkedIn, show a student ID card, or join a short video call.
  • Pushes you aggressively to apply fast or claims "only 2 seats remaining in the quota".
  • Claims to represent the university admissions head office without an official .ac.in / .edu.in email domain.

If you see any of these warning signs, stop immediately and report it to our community.

THE FACE BEHIND ALMMATE

Meet the founder

Ankit Sharma

Founder and Director of ALMMATE

Ankit Sharma is an entrepreneur and the founder of ALMMATE, a student-led community for people choosing and pursuing online degrees in India.

While pursuing his own online BBA, Ankit began his career as an admission counsellor. Working closely with students, he saw a gap that was hard to ignore. Many students were choosing universities on the strength of promises, and many more were left without guidance once admission was complete, facing unanswered calls, unclear processes and nobody to turn to.

Through his YouTube channel, where he reviews online universities as a student, Ankit built a community of learners who shared the same concerns, before and after admission. ALMMATE brings these students together on a single platform, where current students and alumni guide prospective students with honest, first-hand experience.

Today, Ankit leads ALMMATE with one aim: to make online education simpler, more transparent and more reliable for every student who chooses it in the future.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ALMMATE.

Is ALMMATE free to join?

Yes, completely. Joining the community and speaking to an enrolled mentor costs nothing, with zero hidden charges and no consultation fees.

How do I know I’m talking to a real student and not a counsellor?

Check their LinkedIn profile for the university under Education, and ask to see their student ID card or ERP portal live on a short video call. Verified mentors also receive an audited badge from our team.

Do mentors charge anything for advice?

No. ALMMATE mentors never charge for guidance. Mentors participate for networking, community contribution, and leadership recognition — never money.

Is ALMMATE linked to any university?

No. ALMMATE is completely independent and has no commercial partnership with any university. Views shared are personal first-hand experiences of enrolled students.

Do you take commission from universities for admissions?

No. Paid commission is the exact broken incentive we exist to eliminate. We never accept affiliate referral payouts.

Can I join if I haven’t chosen a university yet?

Yes! Share your shortlist of colleges and we’ll introduce you to currently enrolled students from each so you can compare honestly.
